# Refund Policy — Standard Operating Procedures

## Section 1: Refund Timelines by Payment Method

| Payment Method | Standard Refund Timeline | Expedited Refund (Agent-Initiated) |
|---|---|---|
| UPI / Net Banking | 5–7 business days | 2–3 business days |
| Credit Card | 7–10 business days | 5–7 business days |
| Debit Card | 5–7 business days | 2–3 business days |
| Flipkart Pay Later | 3–5 business days to credit limit | Not applicable |
| Cash on Delivery | 3–5 business days (bank transfer) | Same day (Flipkart wallet) |

## Section 2: Full Refund vs. Partial Refund

### 2.1 Full Refund Scenarios (Always Approve)

- Item not delivered (OND — Order Not Delivered) confirmed by carrier.
- Wrong item delivered and return initiated.
- Item arrived damaged with photographic evidence.
- Seller-cancelled orders.

### 2.2 Partial Refund Scenarios (Requires Judgment)

- Item partially used before defect found: Refund amount = purchase price minus depreciation estimate.
- Missing accessories (not main item): Refund only for missing accessory value.
- Customer changed their mind post-use: No refund. Offer store credit at 50% of item value.

## Section 3: Compensation Guidelines

### 3.1 Delayed Refund Compensation

If a refund has not been processed within the stated timeline:
- **Delay of 1–3 days:** Apologize and initiate expedited refund. No additional compensation.
- **Delay of 4–7 days:** Rs. 100 Flipkart voucher + expedited refund.
- **Delay above 7 days:** Rs. 200 Flipkart voucher + escalate to finance team immediately.

### 3.2 High-Value Order Compensation

For orders above Rs. 50,000:
- All refund decisions require **Tier 2 review**.
- Customer must be offered a dedicated callback within 2 business hours.
- Standard response: "I am escalating this to our specialist team who will contact you within 2 hours."

## Section 4: Refund Denial Scenarios

- Customer initiated return past all override windows.
- Item shows signs of customer misuse.
- Refund previously processed and confirmed by payment gateway.
