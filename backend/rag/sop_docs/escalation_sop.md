# Escalation SOP — When to Escalate vs. Resolve Directly

## Section 1: Immediate Escalation Triggers (Do Not Attempt Resolution)

The following conditions require immediate escalation to Tier 2 or above. Do not attempt to resolve these unilaterally.

1. **High-Value Orders (> Rs. 50,000):** Any complaint, return, or refund request on orders above this threshold.
2. **Legal / Consumer Forum Threat:** Customer explicitly mentions "consumer court," "legal notice," "NCDRC," or similar.
3. **Fraud Suspicion:** Pattern of multiple returns in a 30-day window (> 3 orders), mismatch between delivery address and known customer address, or customer unable to verify basic account details.
4. **Repeat Escalation:** Customer has contacted support 3+ times for the same order without resolution.
5. **Media / Social Media Threat:** Customer mentions escalating to social media, press, or review platforms.
6. **Seller Dispute:** Complaint is against a third-party seller on the platform (not Flipkart Assured). Route to Seller Grievance team.

## Section 2: Tier 1 Resolvable Scenarios

Tier 1 agents can resolve the following WITHOUT escalation:
- Standard OND (Order Not Delivered) claims where carrier confirms non-delivery and order value ≤ Rs. 50,000.
- Wrong item delivered (exchange/refund for orders ≤ Rs. 50,000).
- Damaged item with photo evidence (orders ≤ Rs. 50,000).
- Late return requests within the 5-day override window.
- Delayed refund complaints where delay is ≤ 7 days.

## Section 3: Tone Calibration by Sentiment

### Highly Distressed / Angry Customer (Sentiment Score < 0.3)

- Begin with empathy acknowledgment before any policy statement.
- Script: "I completely understand how frustrating this must be, and I sincerely apologize for the inconvenience caused."
- Do NOT lead with policy limitations. Lead with what CAN be done.
- If customer uses abusive language: Issue one polite warning. Escalate to supervisor if it continues.

### Neutral Customer (Sentiment Score 0.3–0.7)

- Standard transactional tone. Acknowledge issue, state resolution timeline, confirm next steps.

### Positive / Patient Customer (Sentiment Score > 0.7)

- Acknowledge their patience. Resolve efficiently. Consider proactive compensation (Rs. 50–100 voucher) as a goodwill gesture.

## Section 4: Escalation Handoff Protocol

When escalating to Tier 2:
1. Summarize the case in 2–3 sentences in the ticket notes field.
2. Tag the ticket with the correct escalation category.
3. Inform the customer: "I am escalating this to our specialist team. You will receive an update within [X] hours."
4. Do NOT promise a specific resolution outcome. Only promise a follow-up.
